Molecular Mechanisms

GHK-Cu functions as a potent signaling peptide that activates tissue remodeling genes, upregulating collagen and glycosaminoglycan synthesis while modulating matrix metalloproteinase activity to balance extracellular matrix turnover.

As a copper-chelating molecule, GHK-Cu also exhibits notable antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, supporting research into cellular repair mechanisms following oxidative or mechanical tissue stress.

Clinical Studies Summary

STUDY ID: J-DERM-SCI-2021

GHK-Cu and dermal collagen remodeling in vitro.

Fibroblast cultures exposed to GHK-Cu exhibited measurable increases in Type I and III collagen synthesis alongside elevated antioxidant enzyme activity.

STUDY ID: WOUND-REP-REGEN-2020

Copper peptide complexes and extracellular matrix modulation.

Results indicated favorable modulation of matrix metalloproteinase expression, supporting balanced tissue remodeling during the research wound-healing cascade.
